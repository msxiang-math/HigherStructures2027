Objectives

The conference is devoted to promoting interactions between mathematicians and physicists working in higher structures in geometry and physics. It will bring together specialists from several fields, such as algebraic geometry, differential geometry, homological algebra, integrable systems, etc., to encourage cross-fertilization between different fields, and to promote the dissemination of the most recent results of current research.
